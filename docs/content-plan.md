# Content plan: 300 to 500 verified words

A proposed structure for growing the course from about 50 words to 300–500,
each verified by a native speaker and recorded natively. Topics and counts
only — the words themselves come from your spreadsheets and your reviewer.

## Targets

- **8 to 10 word groups per lesson** — short enough to finish in one sitting,
  long enough to be worth unlocking.
- **2 to 3 lessons per unit**, one topic each.
- 300 words ≈ **32–38 lessons** ≈ **14–16 units**; 500 words ≈ **50–60
  lessons** ≈ **20–24 units**.
- A gendered word (masculine + feminine) is one word group but two recordings;
  plan recording time by forms, not words (roughly 1.1–1.2 forms per word).

Ordered for the three audiences — travellers, family, and the diaspora
reconnecting with Tunisian. Counts are word groups.

and search "Ask for this word" show it).

## Notes for the current workbook (v2)

- It covers most of units 1–15 and 17–20 above with **283 word groups in 19
  lessons**. Several lessons are well over the 8–10 target (Common Verbs 26,
  Family 20, Food 20, Getting Around 19). Splitting them into more
  `lesson_number`s in a later version of the sheet would keep lessons short;
  the importer handles several lessons per unit.
- 12 of its words also exist in the Phrasebook (hello, thank you, please…).
  That's fine — they're separate features — but your reviewer may want both
  versions to match.
- Words a native speaker hasn't confirmed stay hidden from learners, and a
  lesson appears only once it has approved words — so the course can grow
  lesson by lesson as reviews come back.
